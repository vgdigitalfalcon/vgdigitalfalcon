FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I become an RMB member?

To become an RMB member, you must first be an active Rotarian. You can apply through our membership portal or contact our team directly.

Who can join RMB Chennai Central?

Membership is open to active Rotarians in good standing who are business owners, professionals, or entrepreneurs.

How do I attend an event?

You can register for upcoming events through our Newsroom or Events page. Members get priority booking.

Where is the office located?

Our office is at Rotary House, 122, Anna Salai, Chennai - 600002, Tamil Nadu, India. See the map above.

How can I contact the committee?

Use the contact form above, email chennaicentralrmb@gmail.com, or call +91 95662 44450 (9 AM - 5 PM).

How can I stay updated?

Check our Newsroom regularly and follow us on social media for real-time updates and newsletters.

RMB Chennai Central is part of Rotary International, a global network of business and professional leaders united by a commitment to service and ethical leadership.
