Frequently Asked Questions
What is ACP cladding?

Aluminum Composite Panel (ACP) cladding is a modern material used for building exteriors, providing a sleek look and weather resistance.

Do you provide installation services?

Yes, we provide end-to-end installation services with our expert in-house team to ensure precise and safe fitting.

How long does installation take?

The timeline depends on the project size. However, we always strive to meet agreed-upon deadlines without compromising quality.

Which cladding material is best?

The best material depends on your specific requirements, budget, and aesthetic goals. We offer consultation to help you choose.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes, all our cladding solutions come with standard manufacturer warranties, and we guarantee our workmanship.

Do you handle turnkey projects?

Absolutely. We manage everything from initial design and material procurement to final execution and handover.
