Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

What is Exterior Glazing?

Exterior Glazing is a protective outer layer applied to the exterior walls of a building. It shields the structure from weather elements, improves thermal performance, and enhances the visual appearance of the façade.

Which cladding material is best for buildings?

The best material depends on the building type, climate, budget and design goals. Common options include ACP (Aluminium Composite Panels), high-pressure laminates, fibre cement, natural stone and metal cladding. Our experts help you choose the ideal solution.

Does cladding improve energy efficiency?

Yes. Quality Exterior Glazing systems provide excellent thermal insulation, reducing heat transfer and helping maintain indoor temperatures, which lowers energy consumption for heating and cooling.

Is Exterior Glazing suitable for all buildings?

Exterior Glazing is suitable for a wide range of buildings including commercial, residential, industrial, retail, institutional and hospitality projects. We assess each project to recommend the most appropriate system.

Do you provide installation services?

Yes, we offer complete professional installation services carried out by our trained and experienced team, ensuring quality workmanship and adherence to safety standards.

How long does Exterior Glazing last?

With proper material selection and professional installation, Exterior Glazing can last 20–30 years or more, depending on the material, environmental conditions and maintenance.
