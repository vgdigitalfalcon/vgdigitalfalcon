Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

What is ACP cladding?

ACP (Aluminium Composite Panel) cladding is a lightweight, durable exterior finishing system made of two aluminium sheets bonded to a core material. It offers excellent aesthetics, weather resistance and energy efficiency for modern buildings.

Is ACP cladding fire resistant?

Yes, we supply fire-rated ACP panels that meet stringent fire safety standards. The core materials are selected to provide high fire resistance and protect the building structure.

What thickness options are available?

ACP panels are commonly available in thicknesses from 3mm to 6mm. Our experts recommend the ideal thickness based on the application, structural requirements and design specifications.

Where can ACP cladding be used?

ACP cladding is ideal for commercial buildings, retail spaces, hospitals, educational institutions, corporate offices, residential complexes and many other architectural applications requiring modern aesthetics and protection.

Do you provide installation services?

Yes, we provide complete end-to-end installation services with our trained in-house team to ensure precise, safe and high-quality fitting of all ACP cladding solutions.

How long does installation take?

The timeline depends on the project size and complexity. After site assessment we provide a clear schedule and always strive to complete installation on time without compromising quality or safety.
