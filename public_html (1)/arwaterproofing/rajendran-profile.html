ABOUT THE FOUNDER

C. Rajendran

C. Rajendran founded A.R. Waterproofing in 1995 and has led the company for over three decades from its base in Nungambakkam, Chennai. Under his leadership, the firm has grown into a trusted name for waterproofing and construction solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial properties.

Known for his practical approach and commitment to quality, he personally oversees key projects and maintains a strong focus on long-lasting results. As a member of the Rotary Club, he also actively contributes to community service alongside his professional work.

With a skilled team of engineers and technicians, A.R. Waterproofing has completed 1000+ projects and served 800+ satisfied clients. Rajendran’s mission remains clear: to provide affordable, reliable, and durable waterproofing solutions that protect buildings from water damage for years to come.
